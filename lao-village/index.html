Village of Words

You wake beneath a stilt house in a Lao village on the Mekong. You don't know how you got here. In your hand is a stone carved with letters you can't yet read.

Earn kip, shop at the market, ride a motorbike to town, and find the hidden word stones.

Drag the left side to walk, the right side to look. Tap words to hear them. Speak Khmer words to work magic. Headphones help.
